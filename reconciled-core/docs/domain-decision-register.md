Product approval is separate from implementation acceptance. The [policy document](domain-decisions-v1.0.md) identifies rules not yet enforced by migrations V001–V005. Monetary thresholds, quorum details, retention and residency require each tenant's accountable officers to configure and approve their settings. Do not use the current schema candidate as a production approval system until the release blockers and acceptance tests are complete.
