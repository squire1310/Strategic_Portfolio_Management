# Reconciliation required before deployment

The repository's existing `docs/solution-overview.md` identifies the approved native core as:

| Module | Approved responsibility in solution overview | Responsibility in this draft |
|---|---|---|
| M01 | Strategy & Outcomes | Strategy and measures; partial match |
| M02 | Portfolio Architecture | Intake; mismatch |
| M03 | Demand & Business Cases | Portfolio and initiative; mismatch |
| M04 | Prioritisation & Selection | Finance; mismatch |
| M05 | Investment Decisions & Commitments | Capacity; mismatch |
| M06 | Governance & Assurance | Governance decisions and baselines; partial match |

Finance and resources are M07 and M08 in the overview, not M04 and M05. This draft also lacks first-class business-case revisions, scoring models/scenarios, investment commitments, and assurance findings. The current SQL therefore **must not be presented as the implementation of canonical model v0.3 or deployed as the approved M01–M06 core**. It is preserved as editable working material pending a full model reconciliation and PostgreSQL execution test.
