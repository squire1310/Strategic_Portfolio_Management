# Requirements

Store editable Strategic Portfolio Management requirements in this folder. Keep each requirement traceable to its source, owner, status, acceptance criteria, and the relevant module (M01–M12 or Platform).

- [User stories](user-stories.md): role-based stories for the native core, platform, and later extensions, each with acceptance criteria.
- [Build increment 1](build-increment-1.md): product-owner priority US-001–US-004, first-environment decisions and increment exit evidence.
- [Functional requirements](functional-requirements.md): testable behaviours for M01–M06, platform and optional extensions, traced to approved product decisions and official UK government guidance.
- [Non-functional requirements](non-functional-requirements.md): service quality requirements adapted from Defra's public NFR catalogue, with evidence methods and pending tenant targets.

Current design sources are the [canonical domain model v1.1](../reconciled-core/docs/canonical-domain-model-v1.1.md), which adds M06 risk entities, and approved [domain decisions v1.0](../reconciled-core/docs/domain-decisions-v1.0.md). The risk schema is not yet implemented. Other implementation gaps are tracked in [GitHub issue #4](https://github.com/squire1310/Strategic_Portfolio_Management/issues/4).
